# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the community and the standard. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), using the contact details on their GitHub profile. A dedicated contact address will be published before the first release. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning or a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
